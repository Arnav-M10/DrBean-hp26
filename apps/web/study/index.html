No account or key. Each browser gets its own private workspace.

Bring files from any field

Up to five files at a time: CSV, TSV, JSON, text, Markdown, PDFs of up to 20 pages, and PNG or JPEG images. Dr. Bean offers a check only when a table in them fits one of its models.

Answers that quote your files

Gemini reads only the files you approve, once you tick the consent box. Each claim comes with the exact words it rests on, and the server looks for every quote in your files. Any quote it cannot find is flagged.

The assistant’s answers are AI readings and are never shown as checked.

Exact checks by Counterdex

When a table fits a model it can check, Counterdex looks for a curve that passes within every reading’s allowance but breaks your claim. It works in exact fractions, so a curve it finds proves the readings do not settle the claim.

Nothing runs until you approve it

        
Every check, comparison and assistant reading needs its own approval. An exact check keeps its inputs, approvals and proofs as files with SHA-256 fingerprints, plus one readable report you can print or send.
