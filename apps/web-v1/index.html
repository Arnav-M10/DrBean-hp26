Question
Do the measurements force that, or does another explanation slip through?

  1. Five readings

    A small concentration study: five measurements over four hours.

  2. Each with a hard allowance

    ±0.50 mg/L is the largest discrepancy you accept for each reading. It is a reviewed bound, not a standard deviation.

  3. One curve fits

    Its elimination rate, ke ≈ 0.128 per hour, sits inside the claimed range of 0.10 to 0.17.

  4. So does another

    This curve also stays within every allowance, but its ke ≈ 0.099 lies outside the claim. The engine checks this exactly: the data do not force the claim.

  5. Where they disagree

    At 8 hours, with a 0.20 mg/L allowance, the two curves predict readings that cannot overlap. One measurement there can tell them apart.

  6. A new reading decides

    A hypothetical 3.27 mg/L keeps the first curve and rules out the second. It does not prove the claim: other curves may still fit.
