## What is implemented

The [compatibility contract](PAPER_SCIENCE_CONTRACT.md) and executable
`counterdex.paper_mapping` helper classify a reviewed problem's scientific
scope, convert explicitly supported units using exact rationals, and check the
oral amplitude substitution without claiming equivalence of coupled physical
parameter domains. Eligibility grants no execution approval, source authenticity
or mathematical result. Source SD/SEM/CI do not become hard bounds. A statistical
interval or hypothetical allowance requires a separately retained user question.

The [synthetic content](PAPER_FIXTURE_SPEC.md) supplies exact canonical and
minutes/ng-per-mL tables. Controlled accepted records reach real discovery and
checking. They have equal mathematical inputs but intentionally distinct source,
projection, candidate and native artifact identities. Changing concentration
`5.70` to `5.30` changes actual computed parameter vectors as well as digests.
Fixture model/domain/claim/time-origin/row semantics and expected normalization
are now checked against the actual application projection, including drift and
consistently reviewed-edit controls.

Design tests use newly discovered pairs, separately reviewed grids, precision
and horizons. They cover checked separation, insufficient precision, constrained
grid, horizon refusal, lower numerical tier and unresolved enclosure. Original
vectors survive every follow-up branch. New controls add both-compatible at the
engine and ordinary HTTP/worker boundaries. Wider separately reviewed allowance
can retain the outside counterexample without distinguishing the pair. A later
excluded candidate never eliminates every outside model; replay never upgrades
an inconclusive result.

## Two-minute feature explanation

The scientific part checks what a reviewed set of measurements establishes
under one explicit two-rate model. A paper can propose the measurements and
question, but a person must accept the transcription, units, time origin,
parameter ranges and hard measurement allowances. Missing assumptions stay
visible. Confidence intervals and error bars keep their original meaning;
they are not silently converted into guaranteed bounds.

Once those inputs are reviewed and separately approved, Counterdex computes
candidate parameter vectors and checks each against every included observation.
A compatible candidate strictly outside the selected closed interval is a
counterexample to that deterministic question under those assumptions. Failure
to find one is inconclusive. It does not establish the paper's claim or prove
the model describes reality.

For two retained candidates, a separately approved design can compare feasible
measurement times and a proposed future hard allowance. Checked separation
concerns that pair on that approved grid. After a new reading is independently
reviewed, the same original vectors are checked at the reading's own time and
allowance. Either, both or neither may remain compatible, and a narrow enclosure
may remain unresolved. Historical evidence stays intact.

The native download retains exact inputs, original candidates and external
identity anchors. A matching CPython 3.11 checker freshly replays the mathematics.
That validates the recorded check, not document authenticity, AI accuracy or
physical measurement. This branch implements and tests that scientific mapping
boundary; the PDF interface and public-paper experience await their owners'
integration and independent combined-candidate acceptance.
